Questions

Does everyone need a Claude subscription?

For the hands-on parts, yes. We recommend a paid Claude plan and help you choose the right one for your team before the training.

Do you train non-technical teams?

Yes. Most of our sessions are for non-technical teams. Claude Code is an optional track for technical people.

Where does the training happen?

At your office, or at our venue in Lisbon. We also run team offsites in Lisbon or Ericeira.

How is it tailored?

We start with a discovery call to learn your goals and tools, then build the sessions around the workflows with the biggest payoff for your team.
