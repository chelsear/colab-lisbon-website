Key Insights

1. Integration is the gap.
Most attendees can use Claude for chat/writing but don't know how to connect it to their existing tools and workflows.
2. They want to leave with something real.
Every single person mentioned wanting a working system, not just knowledge. Build-focused workshop confirmed.
3. Sales & business automation is hot.
Multiple attendees want sales agents, lead handling, and business process automation. Consider a sales-focused track.
4. Expert validation matters.
Asaf specifically wants expert approval of his work. Build in review/feedback sessions where instructors validate builds.
5. One ChatGPT convert needs Claude onboarding.
Jessica is coming from ChatGPT. Briefly cover Claude's unique strengths vs. GPT (artifacts, projects, longer context, coding).
